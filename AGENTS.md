# Repository guidance

- Keep this a lightweight static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site; avoid frameworks and dependencies unless explicitly requested.
- Use `https://foodsafetygroundwork.com` as the canonical production domain (non-`www`, HTTPS).
- Use `contact@foodsafetygroundwork.com` as the public business email.
- Preserve the existing visual identity, branding, layout, and navigation.
- Do not invent or expand business claims, credentials, certifications, testimonials, customers, locations, statistics, or services.
- Do not alter search-engine verification tags or files without explicit instruction.
- Do not modify DNS, domain hosting, search-console, webmaster-tools, or email systems.
- Primary public pages are `index.html`, `services.html`, `standards.html`, `industries.html`, `food-safety-plans-haccp.html`, `food-safety-quality-management-systems.html`, `about.html`, `resources.html`, and `contact.html`.
- Before committing, validate HTML structure and metadata, check internal links and fragments, confirm canonical/Open Graph/sitemap consistency, and review common mobile widths for overflow and keyboard accessibility.
